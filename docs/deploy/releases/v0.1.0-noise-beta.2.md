# dilla v0.1.0-noise-beta.2

## What's new

- The member sidebar shows Online, Idle and Do Not Disturb members under their highest displayed role. Offline and Invisible members appear together at the bottom. The phone member sheet uses the same grouping.
- Choose Online, Idle, Do Not Disturb or Invisible from the desktop account area or the phone You tab. The choice follows the account across devices and reloads. Online becomes Idle after ten minutes without activity on any connected device; a manually chosen Idle stays Idle.
- Do Not Disturb stops desktop notifications and incoming-call ring sounds on every device. Messages, badges and in-app call banners remain available. Invisible hides presence from other members, but does not hide typing or voice participation.

## Operator notes

- The server migrates its database from schema 18 to 19, adding `user_presence`. Take and verify a backup before upgrading. No browser store or encryption format changes are made.
- This tag retains the opt-in Automatic noise suppression beta from `v0.1.0-noise-beta.1`, with its existing limitations.
- Ask members to reload open tabs. Presence is shared only among members of the same community and loads again after reconnect.
- To roll back to `v0.1.0-noise-beta.1`, stop dilla, use this release's binary to run `dillad migrate down --to=18 --i-know-what-i-am-doing`, then install the older release. Going down drops saved status choices; every account returns to the Online default on a later upgrade.
