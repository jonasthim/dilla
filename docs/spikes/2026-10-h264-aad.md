- Box: CachyOS, kernel 7.2.7-1-cachyos; Node 24.15.0; `GOMAXPROCS=4`; Playwright 1.63.0, Chromium 153.0.8010.12 (`browser.version()`; Chrome for Testing, OpenH264 2.6, software, per its own log line "OpenH264 version is 2.6"); LiveKit v1.13.7 in process (`dilla-testhost -sfu`)
- Rig: `e2e/spikes/h264-aad.spec.ts`; two Chromium pages, H.264 by `publish({ videoCodec: 'h264' })`; the stub worker's `log-h264` mode logs the canonical prefix (NALs before the first slice NAL, its header, the slice header through `pic_parameter_set_id`, every start code `00 00 00 01`), its SHA-256, `frame.type` and the SPS VUI fields; pairs by RTP timestamp after the downtrack offset; a third page joining and leaving to drive key frames; Chromium's own log (`--vmodule=*h264*=3`) grepped.
- Log capture: libwebrtc runs in the renderer, and a renderer never writes to `--log-file`. The first run used `--enable-logging --log-file=…`. Its log held only browser-process lines (portal, capture-device ranking, GCM, the audio service) and no line from any `third_party/webrtc` file, although OpenH264 encoded about 3 300 frames. Its zero "Failed to parse PPS id" count was therefore not evidence. A standalone probe (one page, two `RTCPeerConnection`s, H.264) showed the same split under `--vmodule=*h264*=3,*/webrtc/*=3`: in file mode, no libwebrtc line arrived in 5 s of H.264 (150 frames decoded). Under `--enable-logging=stderr` with `*/webrtc/*=1`, read through Playwright's `DEBUG=pw:browser`, 900 lines arrived, including the `h264.cc` and `h264_encoder_impl.cc` lines. The spec now launches Chromium through a wrapper that appends Chromium's stderr to the log (`--enable-logging=stderr`). It also counts `h264SourceLines` (lines from a `third_party/webrtc/…h264…` source) as a positive control and asserts that count is above 0. The table below is the second run, made with that spec.

### Also seen in the Chromium log

Twelve `ERROR:…/video_rtp_depacketizer_h264.cc:86] Incorrect StapA packet.` lines appeared: six at the end of each test, about 33 ms apart, in one renderer, at the moment the pages closed. They match the SFU's close-time blank frames. livekit-server v1.13.7 `pkg/sfu/downtrack.go` writes `int(30 * RTPBlankFramesCloseSeconds = 0.2)` = 6 H.264 blank frames at 33 ms intervals (`writeBlankFrameRTP`, :1794–1835). Each one is a STAP-A of the 2×2 SPS/PPS/IDR with the room trailer appended after the last aggregated NAL (`getH264BlankFrame`, :1961–1976; `maybeAddTrailer`, :1906–1914). The trailing bytes do not parse as a further STAP-A unit, so libwebrtc drops the packet before the receive transform. No such frame reached the stub's decode log: its counts match the sender's. These are frames the sender never produced, so they say nothing about the sender's prefix. Attributing the error to the trailer is an inference from source; the payload was not captured. It does matter for SP-13 (task 16): in Chromium, an H.264 SFU-injected frame is dropped by the depacketizer and never reaches the transform as a SIF.
